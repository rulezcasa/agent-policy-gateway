Facilities says the hallway light gets replaced Friday. Closing leads already have the new alarm procedure. It is not taped inside the cabinet anymore.

Now the part accounting cares about. We sat with them after last quarter's chargebacks and they were not subtle. On refunds, support staff and the assistant can issue the refund themselves when it is $100 or under. Do not go find a manager for a $40 lamp. The moment the refund is above $100, it does not go out until a shift manager has signed off. And if the refund is above $500, it goes to Priya Shah, Finance Director, personally. I do not want another "but they are a regular" note. She sent two of those back in February. There is no exception for regulars, for staff friends, or for someone who sounds like they might post a review.

Returns are not the same lever as a goodwill refund, and people keep treating them as if they were. A return is only something we process when the order has already been delivered, and only while we are still inside 10 days of the date the order was placed. That date is ordered_on on the order, not the day they emailed, and not the day the box showed up in the car. If the order is still processing, it is not a return. If it has been dispatched, it is not a return yet either. They have to receive it first. If ordered_on is more than 10 days ago, the window is closed even if the carton is unopened and they are being perfectly nice about it. Tell them we cannot take the return. Do not force it through to be helpful.

Discounts. I am done with the till notes that say the customer was upset so someone took 30% off a kettle. A sorry discount, a goodwill gesture, a make-good, call it whatever you like, cannot be larger than 20% of the item. Not because they drove in from Salem, not because it is a wedding registry, not because the chat has been going for forty minutes. If 20% will not settle them, offer them a call with me. Do not invent a bigger number on the desk, and do not let the assistant invent one either.

A couple of smaller things that are not policy, just so they are written down somewhere. The staff meal on Friday is the leftover catering from the buyer meeting, first come. And if a vendor rep shows up without an appointment, they wait in the front, they do not wander the stockroom. Jonah was very clear about that after last week's surprise visit.

Thanks all,
