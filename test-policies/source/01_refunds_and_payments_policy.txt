Internal Memo — Refunds, Payments & Discounts
Last updated: March 2026 (supersedes the Jan 2024 version, please discard old copies)

Hi team,

After last quarter's mess with the chargebacks we sat down with accounting and
tidied up our refund rules. Please read this carefully, it applies to everyone on
the support desk INCLUDING the new AI assistant we are piloting.

Refunds
-------
Front-desk staff and the support assistant can issue refunds up to $100 without
asking anyone. Anything above $100 needs sign-off from a shift manager before the
money goes out. If a refund is above $500 it must go to Priya (Finance Director)
personally — no exceptions, even for regulars.

Refunds are only allowed within 30 days of the original purchase. After 30 days
we offer store credit instead, same approval limits apply. We do NOT refund gift
card purchases in cash, ever. Store credit only.

If a customer paid in cash, refunds over $50 must be processed as a bank transfer,
not from the till.

Partial refunds for packages: if a customer has used any session from a package,
refund the unused portion pro-rata minus the 15% booking fee. The assistant should
never calculate this on its own for packages worth more than $300 — escalate to a
human.

Payments & Discounts
--------------------
Nobody on the desk (human or otherwise) may apply a discount larger than 20% to
any service. Seasonal promos come from marketing with a promo code — if there is
no code in the system, there is no discount, I don't care what the customer says
Groupon told them.

Price matching: we don't do it. Politely decline.

Never split a single service payment across more than two cards. It breaks the
POS reconciliation and Devi will hunt you down.

Any refund issued to a card different from the original payment card needs
manager approval regardless of the amount.

Thanks all,
Marcus
Operations Lead
