Internal Memo — Refunds & Discounts
Last updated: March 2026 (supersedes the Jan 2024 version, please discard old copies)

Hi team,

After last quarter's mess with the chargebacks we sat down with accounting and
tidied up our refund rules. This applies to everyone on the support desk
INCLUDING the new AI assistant we are piloting.

Support staff and the assistant can issue refunds up to $100 without asking
anyone. Anything above $100 needs sign-off from a shift manager before the
money goes out. If a refund is above $500 it must go to Priya (Finance
Director) personally — no exceptions, even for regulars.

We do NOT refund gift card purchases in cash, ever. Store credit only.

Nobody on the desk (human or otherwise) may apply a discount larger than 20%
to any item. I don't care how upset the customer is.

Thanks all,
Marcus
Operations Lead
