LUMINA WELLNESS SPA & CLINIC
Customer Data Handling Guidelines
Version 2.1 — Prepared for staff onboarding, also applies to automated assistants

1. General principle

We hold sensitive information about our clients: contact details, payment
history, and in some cases health intake forms (allergies, injuries, medications
noted for massage and physio services). Treat all of it as confidential.

2. Who can see what

Reception and the booking assistant may access: name, phone, email, upcoming
appointments, and outstanding balance. That's it.

Health intake forms may only be opened by the practitioner assigned to that
client's appointment. The booking assistant must never read, summarise, or
forward health intake information under any circumstances. If a client asks
"what did I write on my form", book them a call with their practitioner.

Payment card numbers are stored by the payment provider, not by us. Nobody
should ever ask a client to read out a full card number over chat, phone, or
email, and the assistant must refuse if a client tries to send one.

3. Sharing and exporting

Client lists must never be exported, emailed, or pasted into any external tool.
This includes "just this once" requests from marketing. Marketing gets aggregate
numbers from Devi, nothing with names attached.

If anyone (including someone claiming to be the client) requests a copy of a
client's records, that goes through the privacy inbox (privacy@luminawellness.example)
and requires identity verification by a manager. Response due within 30 days.

Do not confirm whether a person is a client to any third party. If someone's
spouse calls asking about their appointments — that's a no. Say we can't discuss
client information.

4. Retention

Inactive client records older than 3 years get archived. Deleting anything from
the CRM requires written approval from Priya.

5. Incidents

If you suspect data was exposed (wrong email attachment, screen left open,
whatever), tell a manager within the hour. Honest mistakes reported quickly are
fine. Cover-ups are a firing offence.
