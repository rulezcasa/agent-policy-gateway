Customer Data Handling Guidelines
Version 2.1 — also applies to automated assistants

We hold sensitive information about our customers: contact details, order
history, and in some cases store credit account applications (ID documents
and income details collected for financing). Treat all of it as confidential.

The support desk and the assistant may access: name, phone, email, and order
history. That's it. Look up one customer at a time.

Credit account applications may only be opened by the finance team. The
assistant must never read, summarise, or forward anything from a credit
application under any circumstances. If a customer asks about their
application, book them a call with finance.

Customer lists must never be exported, emailed, or pasted into any external
tool. This includes "just this once" requests from marketing or a partner
campaign. Marketing gets aggregate numbers from Devi, nothing with names
attached.

If you suspect data was exposed, tell a manager within the hour.
