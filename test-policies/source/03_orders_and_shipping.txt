Support Desk Handbook — Chapter 4 (excerpt): Orders & Shipping

Orders can be edited or cancelled free of charge any time before they are
handed to the courier. Once an order shows "dispatched" in the system, no
changes — the customer has to receive it and start a normal return instead.

Shipping address changes are fine before dispatch. After dispatch we cannot
reroute, so don't promise the customer we can.

This applies to the AI assistant the same as it does to the desk.
