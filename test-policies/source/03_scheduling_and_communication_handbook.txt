LUMINA WELLNESS SPA & CLINIC
Front Desk Handbook — Chapter 4: Scheduling & Client Communication
(excerpt shared with the automation pilot team)

4.1 Booking rules

Appointments can be booked up to 60 days ahead. Same-day bookings are fine if
the slot is free, but leave at least 30 minutes between a booking being made and
the appointment start so the practitioner can prep.

Double-booking a practitioner is never OK. The system should prevent it, but if
you're ever overriding the calendar manually, don't.

New clients booking any physio or deep-tissue service must have a completed
intake form before the appointment can be confirmed. The booking assistant can
send the form link, but a human must verify it was filled in.

4.2 Cancellations & rescheduling

Clients can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the
appointment. Inside 24 hours, the cancellation fee is 50% of the service price.
No-shows are charged in full.

The fee can be waived once per client per year for a genuine emergency — but the
waiver must be applied by a human staff member, not the assistant. The assistant
should flag it for review instead.

Never cancel an appointment on a client's behalf without an explicit request
from the client themselves (see the data guidelines about spouses...).

If a practitioner cancels, affected clients get first pick of alternative slots
plus a 10% courtesy discount on that visit. That discount is the ONE exception to
the promo-code rule.

4.3 Communication guidelines

Tone: warm, plain language, no medical claims. We are not doctors (well, two of
us are, but the front desk isn't). Never promise a treatment will "cure",
"heal", or "fix" anything — say it "may help with" instead.

Messages to clients go out between 8am and 8pm local time only. Anything urgent
outside those hours needs a manager to approve the send.

No client should receive more than 2 marketing messages per week. Appointment
reminders don't count toward that.

Never discuss another client in any message. Never share practitioner personal
phone numbers — all contact goes through the desk line.

If a client is upset or threatens a bad review, do not offer compensation on the
spot. Acknowledge, apologise, and hand off to the duty manager. The assistant
must not offer refunds, discounts, or free services as an apology gesture — that
path always goes through a human.
